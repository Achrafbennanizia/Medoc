Timelines

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on what you need built, how many people will use it and which systems it must connect to.

After the free call, we give you a written timeline with the quote, broken into milestones, so you know what arrives when.

What if the timeline slips?

We tell you as soon as we see a risk, not on the day of the deadline.

We explain the cause, what it changes and what we propose. Any change to the plan is agreed with you first.

Payment

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so we give you a fixed quote after the free call.

The quote lists the scope, what will be delivered and the price. The price does not change unless you approve a change of scope.

How do payments work?

In stages, each tied to a milestone you can see, such as an approved design or a delivered version.

The stages and amounts are agreed in writing before work starts.

Are there hidden costs?

No. The quote says what is included.

If something extra comes up, we explain it and give you the price first. Nothing extra is billed without your written approval.

What if I want to change something during the project?

That is normal. Tell us, and we estimate what the change means for time and cost.

You decide. The change goes ahead only once you approve it in writing.

Code ownership

Who owns the code?

You do, once the project is paid.

We transfer the source code to you, together with the documentation you need to run and maintain it.

Can another developer take over later?

Yes. You have the code and the documentation, so any developer can continue the work.

We do not lock you in.

What about open-source libraries and other third-party code?

Most software builds on libraries written by others. We respect their licences and tell you which ones your project uses.

Those libraries stay under their own licences. Everything we write for you is yours.

Will you reuse my material for other clients?

No. We never reuse your confidential material, your data or your designs for anyone else.

Support and warranty

What happens after launch?

Every project comes with a 30-day warranty after launch. If a defect shows up in that time, we fix it at no cost.

What counts as a defect?

Something that does not work as agreed in the written scope.

New features and changes are not defects. We quote them first, and you decide.

And after the 30 days?

Support is optional. If you want us to keep your software up to date and fix problems, we agree a support plan in writing.

If not, you have the code and the documentation, and you are free to go your own way.

NDA and confidentiality

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Ask for one before you share any details, and we sign it before the conversation goes further.

What do you do with what I tell you?

We use it only for your project and keep access to the people who need it.

We never share project details without your permission.

Will you show my project in your portfolio?

Only with your permission. Without it, we do not name you or show your project.

Data protection

How do you handle personal data in a project?

We handle it in a GDPR-compliant way and only process what the project needs.

Before we touch any data, we agree with you how it is shared, where it is stored and who can see it.

How we protect client data

Should I send you real data, such as patient or customer records?

Not by e-mail. If a project needs real or sensitive data, we first agree a secure way to share it, under an NDA if you wish.

Where possible, we work with test data instead.

What happens to what I send through this website?

We use it only to answer you. The privacy policy explains what we receive and how long we keep it.

Working together

Which languages do you work in?

English, French and Arabic. Calls, documents and updates can be in any of the three.

Do we need to meet in person?

Usually not. We work remote first: video calls, shared documents and written updates.

Fewer meetings leave more time for your project.

How often will I hear from you?

At a rhythm we agree at the start, and whenever there is a decision to make or a risk to share.

We reply to messages within 24 hours on working days.
