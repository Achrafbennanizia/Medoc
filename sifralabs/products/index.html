01

MeDoc Dental

Practice management for dental practices. Installed on the practice's own computers, encrypted, and usable without an internet connection.

  • Two jobs, kept apart

    Reception books, bills and files. The medical chart stays with the dentist.

  • Calendar

    Month, week and day views of the same book, with drag-and-drop changes.

  • Patient record

    One file for what the dentist needs to know, with documents kept on the patient.

  • Tooth chart and treatment

    Examine, treat, follow up and correct, on an FDI chart.

  • Prescriptions and certificates

    Printed documents with the lines you reuse every day.

  • Payments and invoices

    Receipts, invoices, the cash book and the day close; supplier orders too.

  • Tasks and validation

    Reception drafts, the dentist validates. A bell when something changes.

  • The practice's numbers

    Statistics on activity and revenue, and working hours for the team.

Not in MeDoc yet

X-ray and imaging links (DICOM), electronic prescriptions, two-factor sign-in and insurance claim forms. They are on the roadmap below; we don't sell them until they ship.
