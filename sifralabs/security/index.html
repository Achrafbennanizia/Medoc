Security

How we look after your data and your code

When you work with us, you trust us with files, access and ideas. This page sets out the precautions we take as a company, from the first call to the end of the project. Our products have their own security pages.

01

What we do with what you share

These rules apply to every project and every conversation, from the first call on.

Only the access a task needs
We ask for the least access that does the job, such as a test account instead of an admin account. When the task is done, we ask you to remove it.
NDA on request
Ask for a non-disclosure agreement before you share details. We sign it before the conversation goes further.
Confidential by default
We use what you share only for your project. We never name you or show your work without your permission.
Your code is yours
Ownership of the code we write for you is transferred to you.
No patient records by e-mail
Please don't send patient records, customer files or passwords by e-mail. We never ask for them that way.
A secure transfer, agreed first
If a project needs real data, we agree with you first how it is shared, where it is stored and who can see it. Where we can, we work with test data instead.
Deleted when you wish
If you wish, we delete the data you gave us when the project ends, and confirm it in writing.
Support without your patients' data
When we help a practice that uses MeDoc, we don't ask for patient data. If we need to see a problem, we look at it with you on a screen you share. Support files stay on your computer until you choose to send them.

This website sets no cookies and runs no trackers or analytics. The contact form forwards your message to our mailbox and keeps no copy.

02

How we build

Security problems are easier to prevent than to repair. These habits are part of every change we make.

  1. Every change is reviewedWe read each change before it ships and check what else it touches.
  2. Tests run on every changeIn our own products, every change runs the automated tests on Linux and Windows, end-to-end tests in a real browser, and a strict linter that fails on any warning.
  3. Dependencies kept in checkWe install exact, locked versions of every library. On every change, an automatic audit checks them against known vulnerabilities.
  4. Tested before deliveryNothing reaches you untested. Defects found in the 30 days after launch are fixed at no cost.

03

What we have not done yet

We are a young company, founded in 2026. We would rather tell you than have you find out.

  • No security certification yet. We hold no security certificate, and no independent penetration test has been run on our products.
  • Our installers are not code-signed yet. Windows and macOS may show a warning when you install our software.

05

Report a vulnerability

Found a security problem in one of our products or websites? Please tell us privately first.

Write to our security address, in English, French or Arabic.

security@sifralabs.com

What this covers

  • sifralabs.com, including its contact form.
  • The MeDoc Dentist website.
  • MeDoc Dentist, the practice program.

A problem in a service we use, such as our hosting or e-mail provider, belongs with that provider.

What to include

  • What you found, and where: the product and its version, or the web address.
  • The steps to reproduce it.
  • What an attacker could do with it.
  • How we can reach you, and whether you want to be named.

If your report contains sensitive details, say so in a short first message. We then agree a secure way for you to send them.

What we do

  • We reply within 24 hours on working days to confirm we have your report.
  • We look into it, then tell you whether we could reproduce it and what we plan to do.
  • We keep you informed until it is fixed, and agree with you when it can be made public.
  • We credit you when the fix ships, if you want to be named.
  • We will not take legal action against research done in good faith under this policy.

We are a young, founder-led company. A complex fix can take time, and we tell you honestly how long we expect it to take.

What we ask of you

  • Never access, change or keep real patient or client data. If you reach any, stop and tell us.
  • Don't disrupt anyone's systems, and don't run denial-of-service tests.
  • Don't use phishing or other social engineering, and don't test physical security.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish.

We don't run a paid bug bounty.
