Security

What we protect, how, and what we haven't done yet.

Health records are among the most sensitive data there is. This page says plainly how our software handles them. Where something is missing, it says that too.

01

How MeDoc Dentist protects patient data

Where the data lives
On the practice's own computers. Patient records are not sent to Sifra Labs or to any cloud. The software works offline.
Encryption at rest
The practice database is encrypted with SQLCipher. A copied disk or a stolen laptop does not expose readable records.
Who sees what
Role-based access, checked in the application core and not only on screen. Reception cannot read the medical chart.
Audit trail
Reads of medical data and sensitive changes are written to an audit log chained with HMAC, so later edits to the log can be detected.
Between devices
Devices in one practice talk over the local network with TLS; clients pin the server's certificate fingerprint.
Backups
Backups include attachments and go to a folder the practice chooses, such as a USB or external drive.
Patients' rights
Built-in export and erasure, plus generators for the processing register and data-protection impact assessment.
Licences
Signed with Ed25519 and bound to the practice's devices.

02

Not done yet

We're working on these. Until they ship, we don't claim them.

  • Two-factor sign-in is built but switched off until it has been fully tested.
  • Installers are not yet code-signed. Windows and macOS may warn at install; signing certificates are in progress.
  • No independent certification or penetration test yet. We map our work to the standards below; we don't hold their certificates.
  • No emergency “break-glass” access to a chart outside the normal roles.

03

The rules we build to

Our requirements and tests are mapped to these standards and laws.

  • IEC 62304 Software lifecycle
  • ISO 14971 Risk management
  • IEC 82304-1 Health software safety
  • ISO/IEC 27001 Information security
  • ISO 27799 Security of health data
  • ISO 9241 Usability
  • WCAG 2.2 Accessibility
  • Loi 09-08 Personal data, Morocco
  • GDPR Personal data, EU

04

Report a vulnerability

If you find a security problem in MeDoc Dentist or on our websites, write to security@sifralabs.com. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.

We will:

  • confirm we received your report within 5 working days;
  • keep you informed while we fix it;
  • credit you when the fix ships, if you want to be named;
  • not take legal action against research done in good faith under this policy.

Please:

  • never access, change or keep real patient data, and stop as soon as you reach any;
  • don't disrupt a practice's systems or run denial-of-service tests;
  • give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish.
