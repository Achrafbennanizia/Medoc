Visiting this site

The site is served by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site against attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type. We don't set cookies, run analytics or advertising, or build profiles of visitors.

The contact form

When you send the form, what you entered (name, organisation, e-mail, phone, topic and message) goes over an encrypted connection to our server on Cloudflare, which forwards it by e-mail to contact@sifralabs.com, hosted by Zoho. The server keeps no copy. We use it only to answer you. If the server can't be reached, your own e-mail app opens with the message ready instead, and you decide whether to send it.

E-mailing us

When you write to one of our addresses, we receive your message, your address and anything you include. We use it only to answer you, and keep it only as long as the conversation, or the law, requires. Our mailboxes are hosted by Zoho Corporation.

Please don't send patient records or other confidential files by e-mail. If a project needs them, we agree a secure way first, under an NDA if you wish.

MeDoc Dentist stores patient data on the practice's own computers. We do not receive it. Each practice is responsible for the patient data it holds; see the MeDoc Dentist privacy documents for details.
