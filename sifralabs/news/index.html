  4. 23 Nov
    MeDoc Dentist

    Automatic backups, much smaller

    MeDoc Dentist backs up the practice once a day by itself. Each backup is compressed, then encrypted, and in our tests it takes up under a third of the space of the encrypted database. The last 3 days, one from last week and one from the last half-year are kept.

    Moroccan invoices, prescriptions and certificates in French or Arabic

    Moroccan invoices print in French or Arabic with your ICE, IF and TP, and in Arabic the layout runs right to left. Prescriptions and medical certificates print in French or Arabic on your letterhead, with the dentist's INPE.

  6. 18 Nov
    MeDoc Dentist

    A new tooth chart for the dental examination

    All 32 permanent teeth in FDI order along a curved arch. Pick a status such as caries, filling, crown, missing or implant and click a tooth. In the examination, statuses can be combined, and contradicting ones are cleared automatically. Every tooth can be reached by keyboard and is named for screen readers.

  8. 12 Nov
    MeDoc Dentist

    Allergies checked before a prescription

    Before a prescription is saved, its active substance is checked against the allergies in the patient's medical history. On a match, the dentist has to confirm before it is issued.
