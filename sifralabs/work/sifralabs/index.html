sifralabs.com and its contact service

A static company site in English, French and Arabic, with no cookies or trackers, and a contact form that falls back to your own mail app.

The problem

We needed a company site in English, French and Arabic that collects nothing it does not need and still gets every message to us.

Each extra language usually means another copy of every page to keep in step. And the contact form is often the weakest part of a small site: it attracts spam, it can send the same message twice, and when the mail server is down the message is lost.

What we built

A static site with one English source for each page. The French and Arabic pages are generated from it every time the site is built.

A contact service that receives the form, checks it and e-mails it to our mailbox. The demo form on the MeDoc Dentist website uses the same service.

A command menu that jumps to any page from the keyboard, and pages that start loading when you point at a link, so the next one opens faster.

How it works

Vite builds plain HTML pages. Shared parts such as the header and the footer are written once and included at build time.

Translations are dictionaries keyed by the English text. The build swaps every sentence, sets Arabic pages to right to left, adds the language links, and lists any sentence that still lacks a translation.

The Content-Security-Policy only allows files from sifralabs.com itself, forbids inline styles, and stops other sites from framing the pages.

Fonts are served from the site too. There are no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

Only the contact API runs code, on a Cloudflare Worker. Every other page is served as a static file.

The Worker accepts messages only from our own sites, limits the size of every field, and strips line breaks from the fields that go into e-mail headers.

Two quiet spam traps sit in the form: a hidden field that people never fill in, and a minimum time on the page.

Each message carries an idempotency key. If you click twice or the browser retries, the Worker recognises the key for 10 minutes and sends the message only once.

The Worker sends the e-mail with Cloudflare Email Sending over an encrypted connection and does not store the message.

If the service can't be reached, your own mail app opens with the message filled in, and a button copies the text in case you prefer to paste it.

Results

  • Three languages from one source, with Arabic laid out right to left.
  • No cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • One contact service for both of our sites, with a fallback when it is down.
  • A double click or a retry sends one message, not two.
  • Security headers on every page: a Content-Security-Policy, HSTS, and a Permissions-Policy that turns off the camera, microphone, location, payment and USB.
  • A security.txt file that tells researchers where to report a vulnerability.
