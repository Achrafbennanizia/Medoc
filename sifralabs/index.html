Sheet 02 · What we do

Many disciplines, one product team

Health software fails at the seams between code, security, law and the people who use it. We treat them as one job.

  • Software & AI engineering

    Desktop and network systems in Rust and TypeScript. AI only where its output can be checked.

  • Security & privacy

    Encryption at rest, roles enforced in the core, audit trails that show tampering.

  • Usability engineering

    Designed with reception staff and clinicians, in their languages, for their busiest hour.

  • Sync & cloud

    Devices in one practice stay in step over the local network. Cloud only as an option.

  • Data & reporting

    The practice's own statistics, computed on its own machines, never pooled with anyone else's.

  • Law & standards

    Loi 09-08, GDPR and health software standards built into the requirements.

Principles

Four promises, each with evidence

A principle is only worth stating if the code proves it.

  1. Data stays where it is created

    Encrypted SQLCipher database on the practice's computers. Works offline.

  2. Every access leaves a trace

    Reception can't open the medical chart. Clinicians' reads go into an HMAC-chained audit log.

  3. Built to be maintained

    Small, regular releases with documented requirements, risks and tests.

  4. Clear to the people who use it

    Four languages, right-to-left Arabic, light and dark, designed around real practice workflows.
