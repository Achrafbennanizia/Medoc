02

Why “Sifra”

  1. صِفر
  2. cifra
  3. chiffre
  4. cipher

The Arabic word for zero, the empty place, is ṣifr. Around 1200, Leonardo of Pisa learned the Hindu–Arabic numerals on the Maghreb coast and carried them into Europe.

The word travelled with the numerals and changed its meaning on the way: first a number, then a secret code.

That is what we want to be, in one word: careful with numbers and careful with secrets.

Before, during and after

What you can expect from us at each stage of a project.

  1. Before we start

    We make sure the project is right before anyone writes code.

    • We listen until we understand the real problem.
    • We check that it is feasible, and tell you if it is not.
    • You get a written scope and a fixed quote, with no hidden costs.
    • We agree what will be delivered, and when.
    • We check together that we are the right fit for you.

  2. While we work

    You see the progress, and you hear about problems early.

    • Regular, honest updates on where things stand.
    • Commitments kept, or a warning in good time.
    • Work delivered in small steps you can try.
    • Quality checked and tested along the way.
    • Your information protected throughout.
    • Problems raised as soon as we see them.
    • Your feedback asked for at every step.
    • Decisions written down, so nothing depends on memory.

  3. After launch

    Launch is the start of the relationship, not the end.

    • Everything agreed is delivered, and the code ownership is transferred to you.
    • Documentation and a proper handover.
    • We show you how to use and maintain what we built.
    • A 30-day warranty: defects found after launch are fixed at no cost.
    • Support continues as agreed.
    • We ask for your feedback and learn from it.
    • The door stays open, without sales pressure.
