How we work

  1. Small releases, often

    We deliver in increments. Each release adds something useful or fixes something we got wrong, and the practice keeps working through every update.

  2. Evidence before claims

    Every promise on our sites points at something the software does today. What is not ready is labelled “planned”.

  3. Engineering with paperwork

    Requirements, risks and tests are documented and traced, the way health software standards ask. It is slower to start and faster to trust.

  4. Many disciplines, one product

    Software and AI engineering, security, usability, law and economics all decide how a practice works. We treat them as one job, not as departments.

03

Why “Sifra”

صِفر (ṣifr) is the Arabic word for zero, the empty place. Around 1200, Leonardo of Pisa learned the Hindu–Arabic numerals in Béjaïa, on the Maghreb coast, and carried them into Europe. The word travelled with them and became cifra, chiffre, Ziffer and cipher: first a number, then a secret code.

That is the company in one word: careful with numbers, careful with secrets, rooted in the Maghreb, built for the world.
