01

Seven steps, one agreed plan

The scope, the price and the timeline are fixed in writing before development starts. Here is what you get at each step.

  1. DiscoveryA free 30-minute call about your problem, not about technology. You get an honest view of whether software can help.
  2. ProposalA written scope, a fixed quote and a timeline with milestones. Nothing starts until you approve it.
  3. DesignWe plan the screens and the structure before any code is written. You review them while changes are still cheap.
  4. DevelopmentWe build in small steps and show you working parts as we go. You get regular, honest progress updates.
  5. TestingWe test every part before delivery. You try it yourself before anything goes live.
  6. LaunchWe put it live with you and hand over the code and the documentation. We show your team how to use it.
  7. SupportA 30-day warranty: defects are fixed at no cost. After that, support is optional and agreed in writing.

02

Before, during and after the project

What you can count on at each stage. It comes straight from the principles we work by.

  • Before we start

    Making sure it is the right project.

    • We understand the real problem before we propose anything.
    • We check that it can be done, and say so if it cannot.
    • You get a clear scope and price, with no hidden costs.
    • We agree what will be delivered, and when.
    • If we are not the right fit, we tell you.

  • While we work

    No surprises, and you stay in control.

    • Regular updates, in plain language.
    • Working parts delivered step by step.
    • Problems and delays raised early, never hidden.
    • Changes and extra costs only with your approval.
    • Your information protected and shared only with your permission.
    • Decisions written down, and your feedback asked for.

  • After launch

    Finishing properly, and staying in touch.

    • Everything we agreed, delivered.
    • The code, the documentation and a clear handover.
    • How to use and maintain it, explained.
    • Defects fixed at no cost for 30 days.
    • We ask for your feedback and learn from it.
    • The door stays open, without sales pressure.
